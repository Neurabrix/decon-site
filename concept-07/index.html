01 / THE CONFERENCE

Better questions.
Better practice.

DECON connects endocrinologists, physicians, researchers and students in one shared forum—turning emerging evidence into clearer clinical decisions.

Founded as the Diabetes Summit in 2014, the conference now spans diabetes, thyroid, obesity, pituitary, bone, reproductive and maternal medicine.

07 / NEED TO KNOW

Quick
answers.

DECON is an annual international conference focused on diabetes, endocrinology and related areas of internal medicine. It began as the Diabetes Summit in 2014.

The programme is designed for physicians, endocrinologists, researchers, allied healthcare professionals, postgraduate trainees and medical students.
